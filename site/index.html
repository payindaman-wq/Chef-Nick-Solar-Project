One central solar, battery and generator system powers the 560 sq ft kitchen now and grows to carry the future house. Panels sit on a ground mount, the electronics live in a small insulated power shed, and a propane generator starts on its own when the batteries run low.

One-line diagram

System at a glance

Solar
15.84 kW

36 × 440 W bifacial panels, one pallet. Ground mount at 45–50° to favor winter sun and shed snow. Four strings of 9, about 400 V on the coldest morning, under the inverter's 480 V limit.

Inverters
12 kW · 24 kW surge

Two EG4 6000XP in parallel, 120/240 V split-phase. If one fails, the other keeps the fridge and freezer running.

Battery
42.9 kWh

Three EG4 WallMount Indoor 14.3 kWh LiFePO4 with built-in heaters. Just under one day of kitchen use; the generator covers the rest. A fourth unit (≈1.25 days) is the first upgrade.

Generator
24 kW propane

Auto-starts when the battery drops to ~25–30% and charges at up to ~13 kW. Expected mainly in December–January and long storms.

Design decisions

  • One system, not two. One generator, one propane supply and one battery bank serve both buildings. The house adds inverters, batteries and panels to the same shed.
  • Propane for cooking and hot water. Keeps the two biggest loads off the batteries and roughly halves system cost.
  • Ground mount. Ideal winter tilt, no roof penetrations on a food business, easy to extend. No rapid-shutdown hardware needed since it is not on a building.
  • Power shed. Keeps batteries, inverter noise and heat out of the kitchen and above freezing in winter.
  • Soft-start well pump. A Grundfos SQ-type pump cuts start-up surge from ~7.5 kW to ~2–3 kW.
  • Upsize propane to 500 gal. A cloudy December week can burn 60–100 gal in the generator on top of cooking.
  • Winter math. Sized to break even at ~3.8 December sun-hours × 75% efficiency. Summer leaves a large surplus for the house phase.

Questions for Nick

Answers to these tighten the sizing and the price.

Business and schedule

  1. What kind of food business is this (catering, commissary, restaurant prep, classes)? Have you talked to the Washoe County Health District yet?
  2. Days per week and hours per day the kitchen will run? Busiest time of day? Any seasonal swings?
  3. When do you need power in the kitchen? Target opening date?
  4. Is there a general contractor or licensed electrician on the kitchen build? (They may need to sign off on the solar too.)

Kitchen equipment

  1. Fridge and freezer: reach-in or chest, how many doors? Any plans for a walk-in? Models if picked.
  2. Hood: length, fan size, is make-up air required? Fire suppression system?
  3. Cooking: propane burners confirmed. Any electric ovens (convection, combi), fryer or griddle?
  4. Dishwasher, ice machine, espresso machine, proofer, sous vide, dehydrator, electric smoker?
  5. Heating and cooling: mini-split, propane heater, or both? Is AC wanted in summer?
  6. Shop tools: welder, air compressor, table saw? Anything on 240 V?
  7. What must never lose power (freezer, fridge, anything else)?

Well and water

  1. Well depth and pump horsepower once the driller knows? Can the driller install a soft-start pump (Grundfos SQ type)?
  2. Pressure tank only, or a storage tank/cistern too?
  3. Will the well also serve irrigation, animals, or the future house?

Propane and generator

  1. OK to upsize to a 500 gal tank, or add a separate tank for the generator? Leased or owned? Which supplier?
  2. Who will run the gas lines (licensed gas fitter)?
  3. Buy the generator used to save $2-4k, or new with a warranty?
  4. Where should the generator sit for noise: how far from the kitchen and the future house?

Site layout

  1. Where can the panels go? Each Phase 1 pallet needs an unshaded area of roughly 70 x 35 ft facing south. Phase 2 needs about the same again.
  2. Where should the power shed go? Distance from shed to kitchen, and shed to the future house?
  3. Where are the septic tank, leach field and well, so trenches avoid them?
  4. Anything that could shade the array later (trees, a barn, the house itself)?
  5. Any deed or HOA restrictions on solar or outbuildings?

Future house

  1. When will the house be built? How many bedrooms and square feet?
  2. Propane for cooking, water heating and heat, or all-electric/heat pump?
  3. Any EV charging, pool, hot tub, or large shop planned? (Each changes Phase 2 size a lot.)
  4. Should we lay empty conduit toward the house site now while the trench is open?

Budget and logistics

  1. Budget for Phase 1? OK to start with 3 batteries and add a 4th later?
  2. Who pays vendors: Nick directly, or through me?
  3. Delivery: can a freight truck reach the site? Is there a forklift or tractor to unload a ~1,500 lb panel pallet?
  4. Internet or cell signal at the site (for remote system monitoring)?
  5. Will the kitchen business (LLC?) buy and own the system? Has your CPA weighed in on bonus depreciation, the 30% business credit (must be running by end of 2027), and what happens when the house connects later?

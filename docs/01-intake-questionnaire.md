# Intake Questionnaire

Answers drive the load calc, sizing, and the one-vs-two system decision. "Unknown" is a valid answer - we will assume and flag it.

## A. Site and jurisdiction
1. Property location (city/state or ZIP) - sets sun hours, snow/wind loads, permitting authority, utility.
2. Is utility power available at the site today? Does Nick want grid-tied, hybrid (grid + battery + gen), or fully off-grid?
3. Is this a licensed commercial kitchen (selling food, health-dept inspected) or personal use?
4. Will it be permitted/inspected? Is a licensed electrician involved for sign-off?

## B. Kitchen and shop loads (biggest cost driver)
5. Appliance list with fuel type (electric / natural gas / propane) and nameplate volts + amps or watts:
   ovens, range/cooktop (induction?), fryers, griddle, dishwasher (high-temp booster?), walk-in or reach-in refrigeration/freezers,
   ice machine, hood exhaust + make-up air, water heater (type), HVAC/mini-split, lighting, shop tools (welder, compressor, saws).
6. Operating schedule: hours/day, days/week, when is peak use (morning prep, evening service)?
7. Critical loads that must never drop (refrigeration, freezer, etc.).

## C. Electrical service
8. Is the new 100 A panel a main service from the utility or a subpanel fed from an existing service? Main service size?
9. Panel brand/model, number of spaces.

## D. Generator
10. Generac model, fuel (NG or LP), tank size, already purchased or not, does it have its own ATS?
11. Desired role: auto-start to charge batteries on low SOC, or carry loads directly?

## E. Array site
12. Roof: material, pitch, direction it faces, usable dimensions, age, shading. Or ground-mount space available?
13. Distances: array to equipment location; kitchen to future house; kitchen to main service / gen.
14. Where can inverter + batteries live (conditioned room, garage, exterior)? Winter low temps?

## F. Future house
15. Timeline, all-electric or gas, planned service size (200 A?), which building would be the "main" one?

## G. Budget and business
16. Budget range and top goal (lowest cost, max independence, outage resilience, minimal gen runtime).
17. Equipment brand preference (EG4, Sol-Ark, Victron, Schneider, etc.)?
18. Quote scope: parts only, or parts + your labor (rate/hours)? Who handles permits/engineering?

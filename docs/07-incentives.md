# Incentives and Subsidies (researched 2026-10-06)

Not tax advice. Nick's CPA makes the final call. Summary of what applies to an off-grid system on rural land in Washoe County, NV.

| Program | Status Oct 2026 | Applies to Nick? | Rough value |
|---|---|---|---|
| Federal residential solar credit (25D) | Ended for systems installed after 2025-12-31 | No (house) | $0 |
| Federal business ITC (48E), solar | Construction begun after 2026-07-04 must be **placed in service by 2027-12-31** | Maybe: kitchen as a business asset, if built in 2027 | Up to 30% of solar + BOS cost |
| Federal business ITC (48E), batteries | Not on the solar phase-out, but foreign-entity (FEOC) content rules apply to projects starting 2026+ | Doubtful with EG4 (China-made cells) | Up to 30% of battery cost if compliant |
| 100% bonus depreciation | Restored permanently for property acquired after 2025-01-19 | Yes, if the kitchen business owns the system | Full system cost deducted in year 1 (value = Nick's tax rate x cost) |
| USDA REAP grant | Grants paused; new rule (effective 2026-10-16) requires 12 months of production before applying, bars >50 kW and ground mounts on cropland | Not now | $0 near-term |
| USDA REAP guaranteed loan | Open through participating lenders | Possible financing, not a subsidy | Lower-rate loan |
| Nevada renewable energy property tax exemption | Active | Yes: the system's added value is exempt from property tax (residential and commercial) | Small annual savings |
| NV Energy rebates / net metering | Grid-connected only | No (off-grid) | $0 |

## Key points
1. **Bonus depreciation is the reliable one.** If the kitchen business buys and owns the system, it can deduct the full cost in year 1. It has no foreign-content restrictions, so the EG4 equipment is fine.
2. **The 30% ITC is possible but tight.** Phase 1 must be running by 2027-12-31, and the equipment must pass the FEOC material-assistance cost test (Notice 2026-15). Cheap China-sourced inverters, batteries and panels likely fail it. Compliant equipment costs more, so compare the premium with the credit before switching.
3. **Mixed use with the house is a trap.** If a business-owned system later powers the house (Phase 2), the personal-use share can reduce depreciation and can trigger ITC recapture within 5 years. Decide ownership and metering with the CPA **before** buying.
4. **Taking the ITC reduces depreciable basis** by half the credit.
